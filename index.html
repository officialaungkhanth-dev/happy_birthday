Happy 20th Birthday

From this moment to forever, May you be happy.
May fortune finds you everytime.

It took time to understand it.
It took time to realize that not everything that breaks is meant to be fixed.
Some things are just meant to be felt,
remembered,
and then softly set down.

I don't carry anger.
I don't look back with regret.
When the dust settled,
the pain faded,
and only gratitude remained.

Grateful that for a moment,
someone stepped into my quiet world and stayed a while.
Grateful that you taught me how to trust,
how to share the thoughts I keep locked away,
and how to let someone truly see me.

Even though our paths divided,
the good parts of what we built still matter.
They helped shape who I am today.
A little wiser.
A little more patient.
Still quiet,
but no longer feeling quite as distant.

I don't expect things to go back to how they were.
I don't need answers or promises.
I just genuinely want the very best for you.

I hope life is gentle with you.
I hope you are surrounding yourself with people who make you laugh,
people who listen when you speak,
and people who value the person you are.

I hope your goals are coming together,
line by line,
just the way you always envisioned.

Today is a reminder of the day you came into this world.
And even from a distance,
in my own quiet way,
I wanted to send a piece of kindness back to you.

Happy Birthday.

I hope this year brings you genuine peace,
endless joy,
and all the light you deserve.

Thank you for being an important part of my story.
I will always root for your happiness,
wherever life takes you.
